# Your website — a simple guide

Welcome! You can update your whole site yourself, no code needed. Everything
happens in your admin dashboard.

## Logging in

Go to **yourdomain.com/admin** and sign in with your email and password.

## Create a gallery and add photos

1. In the left menu, click **Sections** → **Create New**.
2. Give it a **Title** (e.g. "Weddings"). The web address fills in automatically.
3. Choose a **Cover** photo — this is the image people see first.
4. Add a short **Intro** if you like.
5. Under **Photos**, click **Add Photo** for each image and upload it.
6. Click **Save**.

> Tip: export your photos web-sized (about 2000px on the long edge) before
> uploading. They look great and keep your storage free.

## Show a gallery on the home page

The big sliding banner on the home page shows your **featured** galleries.

1. Open a Section.
2. On the right, tick **Featured**.
3. Set **Order** (a number) — lower numbers show first.
4. Save. It now appears in the home banner and links to that gallery.

To remove one from the banner, just untick **Featured**.

## Edit the About page

1. Click **Pages** in the menu.
2. Open the **About** page (or create one with the slug `about`).
3. Edit the text in the content area and **Save**.

## Publish a blog post

1. Click **Blog** → **Create New**.
2. Add a **Title**, a **Cover** image, and write your post in **Body**.
3. Pick a **Date**.
4. Set **Status** to **Published** when you're ready (leave it on **Draft** to
   keep it hidden).
5. Save. Published posts appear at **yourdomain.com/blog**.

## Read messages from your contact form

1. Click **Submissions** in the menu.
2. Each entry shows the sender's name, email, and message.

You also get an email for every message, so you'll never miss one — but this is
your backup record.

## Change your site title, menu, or social links

1. Click **Settings** (the global settings, near the bottom of the menu).
2. Update the **Title**, **Navigation** links, or **Social** links.
3. Save.

## A few good habits

- **Save often.** Changes aren't live until you click Save.
- New content appears on the live site within about a minute.
- Keep your password private — your login is the only one that can edit the site.

That's it. Enjoy your site!
